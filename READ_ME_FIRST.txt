AUREN v1.3.4 — One Voice, Clean Interface

UPLOAD ONLY app.js and voice.js TO YOUR EXISTING GITHUB REPOSITORY ROOT.
Keep index.html, styles.css and every other file unchanged.

What changed:
- AUREN automatically chooses one British voice, preferring locally installed enhanced/premium voices when available.
- No voice picker, pitch slider, speed slider or extra voice controls on the home screen.
- Voice preview and wake activation move under Settings, preserving the golden-orb home screen.
- Wake preference remains saved, but Safari still requires user interaction to grant microphone access after reopening.

IMPORTANT: This improves voice selection and delivery, not the actual speech engine.
A dramatically more human-sounding neural voice would require a separate TTS engine/service or an enhanced voice installed on the Mac. No API keys or paid services are added.

Test the orb, Settings, Hear AUREN, and Hey Auren after deployment.
