AUREN v1.4.5 — interruption preference (combined with v1.4.4)

Upload ONLY app.js and voice.js to the ROOT of your EXISTING AUREN GitHub repository.
Do not delete or replace index.html, styles.css, memory.js, ai.js, or your Cloudflare Worker.

Settings > AUREN voice > Allow interruptions while AUREN is speaking.
Default OFF: conversations around you should not interrupt AUREN because it stops recognition during speech.
ON: best-effort interruptions; Safari may hear AUREN itself or bystanders. No accurate speaker identification.
The choice is saved locally in this browser.

After committing, hard-refresh Safari and test normal Q&A, wake phrase, Settings and the switch.
