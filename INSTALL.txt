AUREN v1.2 — saved wake preference + voice selection

Upload only app.js and voice.js to the ROOT of your existing GitHub repository.
Keep your existing files and Cloudflare Worker. Do not upload this ZIP.

What changed:
- Select a preferred English voice from voices installed or exposed by your browser.
- Remember whether you want Hey Auren mode enabled.
- When you select the saved-wake option, AUREN starts wake listening via that click (browser asks microphone permission if required).
- After a page reload it remembers the choice and displays a resume prompt. Tap the orb or Resume once before listening. Browsers can block unprompted recording; never bypass this.
- Wake detection and continuous voice conversation from v1.1 remain.

To get better voices on a Mac, install or enable system voices in macOS Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content (availability depends on macOS version), then refresh Safari.
No voice service or paid AI model was added; this is browser synthesis voice selection.
