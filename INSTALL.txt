AUREN v1.4.7 — Interrupt Timing Fix

Upload app.js and voice.js to your existing GitHub repository root.

Interruptions must be enabled in Settings. With it off, AUREN intentionally finishes speaking.
When enabled, AUREN attempts to keep listening during its replies; Safari can block simultaneous speech recognition and playback. Early recognition uses two matching interim updates or a wake phrase where supported.
This addresses a specific restart timing bug and is not a guarantee of perfect hands-free barge-in.
