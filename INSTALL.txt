AUREN v0.9 — Continuous Voice + Wake Phrase

UPLOAD ONLY THESE TWO FILES to the ROOT of your EXISTING GitHub repository:
  app.js
  voice.js

Do NOT upload the ZIP itself. Do NOT delete your other files, particularly ai.js, db.js or memory.js.
After the GitHub Pages deployment has a green check, refresh your website.

WHAT IT DOES:
- Tap the golden orb to start a voice conversation. AUREN will listen again after speaking, until about 20 seconds of quiet, or when you stop it.
- Click Enable “Hey Auren” ONCE to authorize foreground wake-word mode. After that, saying “Hey Auren” (or variants recognized by browser transcription) activates a conversation. With wake mode on, silence returns to monitoring the wake phrase.
- AUREN temporarily pauses microphone recognition while its own voice speaks, reducing feedback.
- Browser speech recognition can stop unexpectedly; this implementation attempts to restart in the foreground, but browser support varies.

IMPORTANT LIMITS:
No website can reliably begin microphone capture from absolutely nothing without any initial user permission/gesture; it cannot reliably hear wake words when closed, backgrounded, or phone locked.
The free browser speech recognition may be unreliable, and audio may be handled by the browser's provider. This version does not change the AI brain or the speaking voice quality.
Test first on desktop Safari, then iPhone. There is no guarantee continuous wake mode works on iPhone.
